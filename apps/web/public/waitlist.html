Questions

The short answers.

Still unsure? Send a note. A real person replies.

Do I need ChatGPT or Claude?

No. Manual scheduling is complete without AI. ChatGPT, Claude and other MCP clients are optional if you already work that way.

Can Relay create the content for me?

Relay schedules content you provide or approve. Bring a caption, photo, video or link; your AI can help draft it, but Relay does not invent your offer.

What does it cost?

Affordable per published post, with no monthly seat fee. Failed posts are free. The full price table is on the pricing page.

Which platforms?

Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, X and LinkedIn. Facebook Pages is pending Meta review and is not part of this early-access promise.

When do I get access?

We review requests personally. If the founding batch is full, you join the next-batch list; we email you when a spot opens.
