Questions

The useful answers.

Still unsure? Send a note. We answer personally.

Do I need Claude or ChatGPT to use Relay Cloud?

No. Relay Cloud is a browser app first. The MCP connection is optional if you already work with an AI agent and want it to schedule posts for you.

What can I publish?

Text, links, images, carousels and video to Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, X and LinkedIn. Facebook Pages is built and waiting on Meta review.

How does pricing work?

Prepaid, per post part. Text posts are US$0.02 per platform, per part; video is US$0.04. Long posts split into thread parts and are priced before publishing. Failed posts are free.

Will Relay post without me?

No. Relay only publishes what you compose, schedule or explicitly ask an agent to publish. It does not scrape or auto-post on its own.

Is my account data safe?

Platform connections use OAuth, so Relay never receives your social passwords. API keys are revocable, and each customer workspace is isolated.

When do I get access?

We onboard the first 100 by hand. When your spot opens, we email a private setup link. If the first batch is full, we will open the next one publicly.
