Questions

The short answers.

When am I charged?

Only after a platform accepts a post. If every platform fails, nothing is charged. If one platform succeeds and another fails, only the successful part is charged.

What counts as a part?

One post on one platform is one part. A three-part thread sent to two platforms is six parts. Text parts are US$0.02 each and video parts are US$0.04 each.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You buy prepaid credit and spend it as you post. The founding deal is a one-time bonus, not a subscription.

Why are RM figures estimates?

Billing is in US dollars. The RM figures use a display rate checked on the date shown. Your card issuer may use a different rate.

What happens if a post fails?

You see the real error on the Failed tab. Retry only the platforms that failed; platforms that already succeeded are never sent again.
