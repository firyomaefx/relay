Billing behaviour

Money moves only when a post does.

This is the part most schedulers get wrong. Relay treats every publish as a reservation, then settles it.

01

Reserve

Before a post is attempted, Relay holds the price from your available balance. Scheduled posts hold the price while they wait.

02

Settle

When a platform accepts the post, the hold becomes a ledger charge for each successful part on that platform. No success, no charge.

03

Release

Failed publishes and cancelled scheduled jobs release the unused hold. Your balance is available again.

A billable unit is one post part on one platform. A 3-part thread to 2 platforms is 6 text units ($0.12). A 5-platform single text post is 5 units ($0.10). Starting rates are per unit, not per draft.

FAQ

Questions,
answered plainly.

What exactly am I charged for?

Only successful posts. Relay reserves the price when you publish or schedule, commits the charge when a platform accepts the post, and releases the hold on failure or cancellation.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. The default model is prepaid pay-as-you-go credits with a free-post allowance. Agency arrangements are separate.

How many social accounts can I connect?

Each platform gets 3 account slots after $10 in lifetime paid top-ups, and 10 account slots after $50. The first account on a platform is the baseline; additional slots are cumulative and one-time. Free starter credit does not count.

Does Relay Cloud include automatic replies?

Threads auto-reply unlocks after at least $10 in lifetime paid top-ups, your own DeepSeek API key, and a connected Threads account with read and reply permissions. The key is encrypted per customer; spam and hostile comments are skipped. Agent-called replies work without the unlock.

How do I access the cloud MCP?

A customer-specific API key is required. Cloud access is currently operator-provisioned while Stripe card checkout is being completed. Use the console or contact Relay for access.

Why does the MCP URL show an error in my browser?

/mcp speaks Streamable HTTP MCP, not HTML. A browser request without an API key returns 401. It is working as intended; use an MCP client.

Can I cancel a scheduled post?

Yes, while it is still pending. The reserved amount is released and the post is not published.
