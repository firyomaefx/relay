How it works with your agent
  1. Get your API key and add Relay to your MCP client config.
  2. Your agent validates the post first, then publishes or schedules it.
  3. Relay holds the price, charges only when the platform accepts the post, and releases the hold if it fails or you cancel.

Every charge goes into a ledger you can check.
