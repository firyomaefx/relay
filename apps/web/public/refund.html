Refund Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Relay Cloud is prepaid and charges per post, so most of the time there's nothing to refund. Here's how it works.

Failed and cancelled posts

You're only charged when a platform accepts your post. If a post fails, or you cancel a scheduled post before it runs, the held amount goes straight back to your balance. You don't need to ask.

Unused top-ups

If you top up by mistake and haven't used any of that top-up, ask within 14 days and we'll refund it in full to the card you paid with. Stripe usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show it on your statement.

Partly used credit

Credit you've started using isn't refundable, but it doesn't expire while your account is open.

Account unlocks

The US$9.90 and US$49.90 top-ups unlock extra account slots and Threads auto-reply. If you get a refund for that top-up, the unlock is removed.

Free credit

The US$0.50 starter credit is free and can't be exchanged for cash.

Charges you don't recognise

Card statements show RELAY DUITPOKET. If you see a charge you don't recognise, or think you were charged twice, contact us before raising a dispute with your bank and we'll sort it out.

How to ask

Use the contact page with the email on your account and the date and amount of the payment.
