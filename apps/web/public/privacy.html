Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Relay Cloud is the trading name for this service. This policy explains what Relay Cloud collects, why, and what you can do about it. We follow Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

What we collect

  • Waitlist and contact details: your name, email, country and anything you write in the waitlist or contact form.
  • Account data: your customer record, a hashed copy of your API key (we can't read the key itself), and your credit balance and ledger.
  • Connected platform access: the access tokens each platform gives us when you connect an account. They are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM).
  • Posts and media: the text, images and videos you send for publishing, scheduled posts, and the results the platforms return.
  • Payment records: Stripe tells us a payment's amount, status and reference. Card details stay with Stripe.
  • Technical logs: IP addresses, request times and errors, used for security and fixing problems.

Why we use it

  • To run the service: publish your posts, keep your balance, and show you what was charged.
  • To contact you about your account, such as waitlist approval and payment receipts.
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service secure.

We don't sell your data and we don't use your posts to train AI models.

Public media links

Some platforms (Threads, Instagram and TikTok photo posts) fetch media from a public link. Media you upload for those posts is served from a public URL on relay.duitpoket.fun, so anyone with the link can view it.

Who we share it with

  • The social platforms you choose to post to.
  • Stripe, for card payments.
  • Google Cloud (servers in Singapore) and Cloudflare, for hosting and network.
  • Resend, for sending account emails.
  • DeepSeek, only if you turn on Threads auto-reply with your own DeepSeek key. Comment text is sent to it to draft replies.
  • Authorities, if Malaysian law requires it.

Some of these providers process data outside Malaysia.

How long we keep it

We keep account and ledger records while your account is open and as long as tax and accounting rules require after that. Uploaded media is cleaned up after it's no longer needed for posting. Waitlist entries that don't become accounts can be deleted on request.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, disconnect any platform at any time, or close your account. Use the contact page and we'll reply within 21 days.

Contact

Relay Cloud · privacy requests: https://relay.duitpoket.fun/contact. We reply within 21 days.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top.
